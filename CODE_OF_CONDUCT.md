# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, technically honest community. Participants should be able to contribute without harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code, designs, and mistakes.
- Critique ideas and behavior, not people.
- State uncertainty and distinguish implemented behavior from proposals.
- Protect private source material, security reports, and personal information.
- Give credit for others' work and respect repository licenses.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope while remaining free to propose alternatives constructively.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discriminatory language, or sexualized attention.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or deliberate misrepresentation.
- Publishing another person's private information or source material without permission.
- Pressuring maintainers to disclose security reports or confidential data.
- Using project spaces to promote malware, credential theft, or abuse.
- Retaliation against a person who raises a good-faith conduct or security concern.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release spaces, and other project-representative interactions.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers. Security-sensitive reports should use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not post private evidence publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, request a behavior change, issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban a participant. Enforcement decisions should consider severity, repetition, impact, and the safety of affected people. Reports will be handled with discretion, but absolute confidentiality cannot be promised where legal or platform obligations apply.

Good-faith disagreement, bug reports, and evidence-based technical criticism are not conduct violations.
